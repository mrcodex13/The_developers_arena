# Business Report

## Executive Summary
This capstone project explores customer churn risk using a machine learning workflow that converts historical customer behavior into actionable retention signals.

## Problem Statement
Retaining customers is more cost-effective than acquiring new ones, so a business needs a reliable early-warning system to detect churn risk.

## Business Challenges
- increasing customer acquisition costs
- reduced revenue from churned accounts
- limited visibility into service usage risks
- difficulty prioritizing retention campaigns

## Data Analysis
The analysis showed that customer lifecycle features such as tenure, contract type, payment method, and total charges strongly influence churn behavior.

## Model Results
The selected models were evaluated using accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and ROC-AUC. Random Forest offered the best balance among the tested classifiers.

## Business Recommendations
- offer loyalty incentives during the first few months
- prioritize customers on month-to-month contracts
- strengthen payment automation and retention outreach
- monitor high-charge customers more closely

## Expected Business Impact
A good churn prediction model can help improve retention strategy, raise customer lifetime value, and support targeted preventative action.

## Conclusion
The project demonstrates how data science can convert customer records into measurable business insight and retention planning.
