# Customer Churn Prediction Presentation

## Slide 1: Title
Customer Churn Prediction using Machine Learning

Speaker Notes:
This project focuses on analyzing customer churn and building a prediction system to support business retention.

## Slide 2: Problem Statement
Customer churn reduces recurring revenue and increases acquisition cost.

Speaker Notes:
The business needs a proactive solution to flag at-risk customers before they leave.

## Slide 3: Dataset Overview
The dataset contains customer lifecycle and billing features.

Speaker Notes:
The dataset includes contract type, payment method, service usage, and billing information.

## Slide 4: Exploratory Data Analysis
Visual analysis shows churn patterns by contract, tenure, charges, and billing behavior.

Speaker Notes:
The EDA helps identify customer segments with a higher risk of leaving.

## Slide 5: Data Preprocessing
Cleaning and preprocessing converted raw records into a model-ready format.

Speaker Notes:
We removed duplicates, handled missing values, and encoded categorical variables.

## Slide 6: Feature Engineering
Key features were transformed into a consistent representation for model training.

Speaker Notes:
Scaling and encoding ensure the algorithms receive stable, comparable inputs.

## Slide 7: Models Considered
Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, SVM, and KNN were tested.

Speaker Notes:
Multiple models were evaluated to identify the strongest prediction pattern.

## Slide 8: Model Evaluation
Accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and ROC-AUC were compared.

Speaker Notes:
The evaluation step determines the most reliable model for deployment.

## Slide 9: Hyperparameter Tuning
Random Forest was tuned using GridSearchCV.

Speaker Notes:
Tuning improves predictive strength and stability.

## Slide 10: Business Insights
Customers with short-term contracts and higher billing levels show elevated churn risk.

Speaker Notes:
These insights are useful for customer retention strategy.

## Slide 11: Deployment
The model is deployed as a Flask app with a simple prediction form.

Speaker Notes:
The deployment layer makes the project usable in a business setting.

## Slide 12: Recommendations
Offer incentives, improve onboarding, and monitor high-risk customers.

Speaker Notes:
Business action should follow the model output to improve retention.

## Slide 13: Conclusion
The project demonstrates a scalable end-to-end machine learning workflow.

Speaker Notes:
This repository can serve as a complete capstone submission for a data science portfolio.
